Elevational and trait associations in Arabis alpina

Read manuscript caption

Figure 4. Elevational and trait associations in Arabis alpina. (a) Flowering onset and (b) flowering duration in relation to elevation. (c) Final flowering date in relation to flowering onset. (d) Mean individual seed mass (MISM, mg per seed) and (e) total seed number per plant in relation to elevation. (f) Total seed number per plant in relation to MISM. FIGURE S5

Spearman rank correlations between environmental variables and reproductive traits

Read manuscript caption

Figure S5. Spearman rank correlations between environmental variables and reproductive traits. Cells show Spearman’s ρ. Green denotes positive and brown denotes negative correlations. Soil PC1 summarises standardised soil properties. Onset and duration refer to flowering. T0, time spread (Tg – T0), MGR and GP denote germination onset, spread, mean rate and percentage.
