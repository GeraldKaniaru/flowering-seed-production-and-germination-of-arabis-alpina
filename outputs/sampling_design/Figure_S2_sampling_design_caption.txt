Figure S2. Field sampling of Arabis alpina. (a) The 200-m belt transect followed an established route from 3,000 to 4,800 m. Eighteen elevation bands with two blocks per band and ten plots per block yielded 36 blocks and 360 plots. Triangles show 27 occupied plots in nine blocks at 3,763-4,567 m. (b) Each block contained ten 5 × 5 m plots placed at random coordinate pairs measured from a fixed corner. Plot boundaries were at least 10 m apart and adjacent blocks were separated by at least 20 m. (c) We sampled 581 well-spaced, separately rooted rosettes of varying size. Stolon-connected rosettes could share a genotype. Positions and distances are schematic.
