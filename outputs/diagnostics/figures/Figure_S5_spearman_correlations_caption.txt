Figure S5. Spearman rank correlations between environmental variables and reproductive traits. Cells show Spearman’s ρ. Green denotes positive and brown denotes negative correlations. Soil PC1 summarises standardised soil properties. Onset and duration refer to flowering. T0, time spread (Tg – T0), MGR and GP denote germination onset, spread, mean rate and percentage. Only the lower triangle is shown. Blank cells identify untested pairs: germination responses with MISM or total seed number, and 100-seed mass with variables other than the four germination responses. MISM is mean individual seed mass (mg per seed) and equals 100-seed mass divided by 100, giving a rank correlation of one by construction. Asterisks identify Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted P values (*, P < 0.05; **, P < 0.01; ***, P < 0.001). Unmarked values have adjusted P ≥ 0.05.
