Figure 6. Associations of germination with source elevation and 100-seed mass in Arabis alpina. (a) Elevation-only models. (b) Mass-only and elevation-adjusted mass models. Circles, squares and diamonds distinguish the three model sets in that order. Points are coefficients and bars are 95% confidence intervals. Dashed lines mark zero. Coefficients are per 100 m in (a) and per mg of 100-seed mass in (b). Responses are time to first germination (T0; days), time spread (Tg – T0, days), mean germination rate (MGR, day-1) and germination percentage (GP, coefficients in percentage points). Annotations give coefficients (β), marginal R2 (R2m), conditional R2 (R2c) and significance (ns, P ≥ 0.05, and ***, P < 0.001).
