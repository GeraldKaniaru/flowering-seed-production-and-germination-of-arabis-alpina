Figure S1. Vegetation along the Mount Kenya transect. (a) Lower montane wet forest. (b) Upper montane forest-bamboo transition with Podocarpus spp., Hagenia abyssinica, Yushania alpina and Lobelia bambuseti. (c) Moorland with tussock grasses, sedges and Lobelia keniensis.
