Figure 5. Within- and among-plot associations in Arabis alpina. (a) Change in final flowering date (days) per one-day delay in flowering onset. (b) Change in expected total seed number per plant on the log-link scale per 1 mg increase in mean individual seed mass (MISM, mg per seed). Within-plot coefficients describe associations with deviations from plot means, and among-plot coefficients describe associations with plot means. Points show estimated coefficients (β), and horizontal bars show 95% Wald confidence intervals. Vertical dashed lines mark zero. In (a), a coefficient of one corresponds to unchanged flowering duration, and coefficients below one correspond to shorter duration with later onset. Significance symbols refer to tests against zero (ns, P ≥ 0.05; ***, P < 0.001).
