Figure 4. Elevational and trait associations in Arabis alpina. (a) Flowering onset and (b) flowering duration in relation to elevation. (c) Final flowering date in relation to flowering onset. (d) Mean individual seed mass (MISM, mg per seed) and (e) total seed number per plant in relation to elevation. (f) Total seed number per plant in relation to MISM. Points represent individual plants, lines show model predictions and shaded bands indicate 95% confidence intervals. Solid and dashed lines indicate P < 0.05 and P ≥ 0.05, respectively, for tests of a slope of zero. Slopes (β) are expressed per 100 m increase in elevation (a, b, d, e), per day of later flowering onset (c) or per mg increase in MISM (f). MISM is natural-log-transformed in (d), and seed-number slopes in (e, f) are on the log-link scale. Flowering dates are expressed as days from 1 June 2021 (day 1). In (c), predictions are based on fixed effects with random intercepts set to zero. The reported P value tests a slope of zero. A slope of one corresponds to constant flowering duration. R2m and R2c denote marginal and conditional R2, respectively.
