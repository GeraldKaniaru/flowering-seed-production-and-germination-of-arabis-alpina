Table S2. Environmental sensitivity models, focal coefficients and numerical diagnostics. Each comparison uses the same 581 maternal plants. H denotes the hypothesis. H1, H2 and H4 coefficients describe elevation associations per 100 m with flowering onset, flowering duration and ln(MISM), respectively. H5 coefficients describe seed-number associations on the log-link scale per 100 m. H3 coefficients describe changes in final flowering date per one-day delay in onset. Climate denotes temperature and precipitation entered jointly, and soil denotes soil PC1. β is the focal coefficient, SE its standard error, and CI- and CI+ its lower and upper 95% Wald confidence limits. P0 tests a coefficient of zero. P1 tests an H3 coefficient of one, corresponding to unchanged duration with later onset. Conv., Hess., and SE fin indicate optimiser convergence, a positive-definite Hessian and finite fixed-effect standard errors, respectively. Bound. indicates whether at least one random-intercept variance was near zero. Eligible indicates whether the fit met the numerical criteria for coefficient-level inference. Boundary estimates alone did not exclude a fit. Adjusted H5 estimates are descriptive only because their non-finite standard errors precluded confidence intervals and P values. Dashes indicate unavailable or inapplicable entries. Values are rounded. Table S1 reports model rankings.
