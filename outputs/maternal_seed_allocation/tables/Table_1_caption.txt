Table 1. Mixed-effects estimates for H1-H6 at plant, within-plot and among-plot scales (581 maternal plants). H denotes the hypothesis, β the coefficient, SE its standard error, and CI- and CI+ the lower and upper 95% Wald confidence limits. Coefficients are expressed per 100-m elevation increase for H1, H2, H4 and H5, one-day delay in flowering onset for H3, and 1-mg increase in mean individual seed mass (MISM) for H6. MISM is measured in mg per seed. H4 uses natural-log-transformed MISM, and H5-H6 coefficients are on the log-link scale. Onset and MISM predictors in the primary association models were mean-centred. Deviation terms represent individual values minus their plot means, while mean terms represent plot means. Within-plot and among-plot coefficients were estimated jointly, and their repeated R2 values describe the same model. R2m and R2c denote marginal and conditional R2. All H3 coefficients and confidence limits describe final flowering date. Subtracting one from these values gives the corresponding duration coefficient and confidence limits.
